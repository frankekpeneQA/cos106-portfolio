My Story

My name is Frank Ekara Ekpene. I am a B.Sc. Software Engineering student at Miva Open University. I began this academic journey to strengthen my understanding of technology and develop skills that can help me build a meaningful career in the software industry.

Alongside my studies, I have been exploring Quality Assurance because I enjoy investigating how applications work, checking whether requirements are met and finding ways to prevent defects from affecting users. I am working towards becoming a confident, detail-oriented QA professional.
